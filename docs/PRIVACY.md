# Drift privacy policy

Drift is a web browser. It has no servers of its own and collects no telemetry,
analytics or crash reports.

## Sign in with Google (Drift Sync)

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do:

- Drift asks Google for your name, email address and profile picture (to show who is
  signed in) and for access to **Drift's own app data folder** in your Google Drive
  (`drive.appdata`). Drift cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
- In that folder Drift keeps one file with your Drift data: bookmarks, pinned tabs,
  spaces, open tabs, browsing history and settings, so they are the same on every
  device where you sign in, and backed up.
- This data goes only between your device and your Google account. Nobody else,
  including the Drift developers, receives it.
- Your sign-in is stored on your device, encrypted with your operating system's keychain.
- You can choose what syncs, or sign out, in Settings → Sync and Google. To delete the
  copy in your account: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Drift → Delete hidden
  app data. You can also remove Drift's access at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Drift's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other connections

Drift contacts other servers only for features you use; Settings → Privacy lists each one
(updates from GitHub, ad-block lists, optional language packs, the optional Gecko engine
from Mozilla). Websites you visit see what any browser shows them.
